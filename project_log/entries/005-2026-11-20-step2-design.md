# 005: Step 2 design — rank training examples by attention
Date: 2026-11-20   Follows: [004](004-2026-11-20-step1-result.md)

**What:** A new script, `step2_attention.py`, scores all 60,000 training examples by last-layer attention `k^T q` for one test image.
**Why:** Attention is the simplest reading of the writes. Irie et al. use only attention.
**Decisions:**
- Test image 0 (a 7, classified correctly) — a simple first case.
- Raw attention, with no softmax and no normalization — the same form as the dual form.
- `step1_dual_form.py` now also saves the trained model and the training-image index of each row — step 2 needs both.

**Expected result:** The top examples are 7s with a bias toward thick, bright strokes, because `k^T q` grows with the size of `k`.
